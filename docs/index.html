New here? How much to borrow

BTC to lock is your collateral. The faucet gives 0.05 BTC, so lock about 0.048 and keep a little for gas.

Borrow is the MUSD loan. Mezo's smallest is 1,800. Mezo adds 200 MUSD plus a small fee to the debt, so 1,850 borrowed is about 2,050 owed.

Keep as reserve stays in your vault to rescue the loan later. The rest goes to your wallet.

Aim to open near 180%. Under 150% the loan is too thin and fails. 0.048 BTC, 1,850 borrow, 300 reserve works with the faucet amount.

Mezo's smallest loan is about 2,000 MUSD of debt. Need test BTC? faucet.test.mezo.org
