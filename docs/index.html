How Bulwark works

Borrow and keep a buffer. You open your MUSD loan through your own Bulwark vault, which holds part of the borrowed MUSD as a reserve.
Set your line. Choose a trigger (say 160%) and a target (175%). Both live on-chain, and only you can change them.
Anyone can rescue. When BTC falls below your trigger, any keeper can call rescue. The contract checks the live Mezo price, repays just enough MUSD to reach your target, and pays the keeper up to 2%.
You stay in control. Withdraw the reserve, add BTC, or close the loan whenever you like. Bulwark never takes custody beyond your own vault.
